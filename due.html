What’s due

13 songs you might reasonably expect tonight

Songs you are expecting: ones the band plays often enough to have a habit, which are now a little past it. Measured against each song’s own recent gaps rather than one number for the whole catalog — a staple is late at eight shows and a rarity is not late at eighty. The figure on the right is how far past: 2× means it has now been twice this song’s usual gap, which is printed under it.

How these lists are measured

Being late is not the same as being expected, and more late is not more expected. A song at six times its usual gap is not one anybody is waiting on — it is drifting out of rotation. So past 3.5× a song is slipping rather than due, past 100 shows it is on the shelf, and with no recent habit at all it is out of rotation. All four are below, though the fourth is a count and a door rather than a list: there are more songs in it than in the other three put together, so they have a page to themselves.

None of this knows what the band has planned. A themed night overrides every figure here — the 2021 Halloween runs built around numbers and animals, the elements nights of the first Sphere run, a run played entirely out of one decade — and the theme is usually not public before the show. On a night like that the list below is the wrong question.
